## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo & Shinjuku Nightlife**  
**Morning / Early Afternoon**  
- **Flight suggestion:** From Brisbane (or your nearest international hub) take a **direct flight to Tokyo (Haneda – HND)**. If you prefer a stop‑over, Cathay Pacific’s Brisbane → Hong Kong → Tokyo route is a solid option (≈ 12 h total).  
- **Immigration & Luggage:** Use the **Narita Express (N’EX) or Tokyo Monorail + JR Yamanote Line** to reach Shinjuku (≈ 1 h).  

**Afternoon**  
- Check‑in, drop bags, freshen up.  
- Walk around **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** (peaceful stroll, free entry with passport).  

**Evening**  
- Head to **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane) for yakitori & cheap drinks.  
- Explore **Golden Gai** – a cluster of tiny themed bars (great for a first‑time “Tokyo bar crawl”).  

**Logistics**  
- **JR Pass:** If you’ll travel across regions (Tokyo → Hakone → Kyoto → Osaka), buy a 7‑day **Japan Rail Pass** (¥29,650 ≈ US$210).  
- **Transport Card:** Get a prepaid **Suica** or **Pasmo** card for city subways & buses.
