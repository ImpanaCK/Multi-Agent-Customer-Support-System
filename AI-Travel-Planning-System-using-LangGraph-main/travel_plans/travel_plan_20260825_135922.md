## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY PLAN  

### Day 1 – Arrival & Getting Your Bearings  
**Flight** – *No direct flight info to Paris was supplied.*  
- If you’re flying from **Vladivostok** or **Sydney**, you’ll need an international connection (e.g., Moscow, Doha, Dubai, or Singapore) to Paris‑Charles‑de‑Gaulle (CDG). Book a **single‑ticket itinerary** to avoid luggage hassles.  
- Aim to land **mid‑morning** (≈10 am local) so you can start exploring right away.

**Transport to hotel**  
- **RER B** train from CDG to **Saint‑Lazare** (≈30 min, €10).  
- Or pre‑book a **airport shuttle** (e.g., SuperShuttle) or rideshare (≈€50).  

**Morning – Île de la Cité**  
- Walk to **Notre‑Dame** (exterior; interior still closed for restoration).  
- Visit **Sainte‑Chapelle** (stained‑glass masterpiece – buy a timed ticket).  

**Lunch** – *Latin Quarter*  
- **Le Petit Cler** (classic bistro) or **L’As du Fallafel** (quick, famous falafel).  

**Afternoon – Luxembourg Gardens**  
- Stroll the formal garden, watch the chess players, and pop into the **Luxembourg Museum** if you like art.  

**Evening – Saint‑Germain‑des‑Prés**  
- Dine at **Le Procope** (Paris’ oldest café, historic vibe).  
- After dinner, enjoy a nightcap at **Le Bar du Marché** (cozy wine bar).  

---

### Day 2 – Classic Art & The Eiffel Tower  
**Morning – Louvre**  
- **Skip‑the‑line ticket** (book 2‑3 months ahead).  
- Focus on highlights: **Mona Lisa, Winged Victory, Venus de Milo**, plus a quick look at the **Egyptian Antiquities**.  

**Mid‑day – Tuileries & Orangerie**  
- Walk through the **Tuileries Garden** to the **Musée de l’Orangerie** (Monet’s Water Lilies).  
- Grab a **picnic** from **Carette** (pastries, cheese, fruit) and eat on the garden lawns.  

**Afternoon – Eiffel Tower**  
- Take Metro Line 1 to **Charles de Gaulle‑Étoile**, then Line 6 to **Bir-Hakeim** for a great photo spot.  
- **Pre‑book a summit ticket** for late afternoon (around 5 pm) to catch the golden hour.  

**Evening – Seine River Cruise**  
- **Bateaux‑Mouches** 1‑hour cruise (starts at 7 pm) – perfect for dinner‑time views of illuminated monuments.  

---

### Day 3 – Versailles Day‑Trip  
**Getting there**  
- RER C from **Saint‑Michel – Notre‑Dame** to **Versailles‑Rive‑Gauche** (≈45 min, €7).  

**Morning – Palace**  
- **Buy “Passport” ticket** (palace + gardens + Trianon).  
- Arrive early (9 am) to beat crowds. Tour the **Hall of Mirrors**, **King’s Grand Apartments**, and the **Royal Chapel**.  

**Lunch** – *Versailles town*  
- **La Petite Venise** (Italian‑French fusion) or a quick bite at **La Cour des Senteurs** café.  

**Afternoon – Gardens**  
- Rent a **row‑boat** on the Grand Canal (optional).  
- Walk the **Orangery** and **Marie‑Antoinette’s Estate** (Petit Trianon).  

**Evening – Return to Paris**  
- Freshen up, then head to **Le Marais** for dinner.  
- Recommended: **L’Ange 20** (modern French) or **Chez Janou** (Provençal).  

---

### Day 4 – Bohemian Montmartre & Nightlife  
**Morning – Montmartre**  
- Take Metro Line 2 to **Anvers** or **Abbesses**.  
- Climb (or take the funicular) to **Sacré‑Coeur**; enjoy panoramic city view.  

**Mid‑day – Place du Tertre**  
- Watch artists at work, maybe get a quick portrait.  
- Lunch at **Le Consulat** (historic café).  

**Afternoon – Shopping & Sightseeing**  
- Metro to **Opéra** (Line 3, 7, 8, 9).  
- Explore **Galeries Lafayette** (rooftop view) and **Printemps**.  

**Evening – Showtime**  
- **Moulin Rouge** (cabaret) – book a dinner‑show package (≈€150 per person).  
- If you prefer something low‑key, head back to **Saint‑Germain** for live jazz at **Le Caveau de la Huchette**.  

---

### Day 5 – Art, Coffee, and Departure  
**Morning – Musée d’Orsay**  
- Housed in a former railway station, home to Impressionist masterpieces (Monet, Renoir, Van Gogh).  

**Mid‑day – Seine‑side Walk**  
- Stroll from **Orsay** across **Pont Alexandre III** to the **Champs‑Élysées**.  

**Lunch** – **Café de Flore** (iconic Left‑Bank café).  

**Afternoon – Last‑Minute Shopping**  
- Wander **Le Marais** for boutique gifts (silk scarves, French soaps).  
- If time allows, pop into **Merci** concept store (great for design items).  

**Departure**  
- Retrieve luggage, take **RER B** back to CDG (allow 2 h for security).
